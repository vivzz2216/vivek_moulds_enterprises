Plastic Injection Moulds

We specialize in manufacturing high-precision, multi-cavity injection moulds designed for mass production. Our moulds are built using high-grade tool steel to ensure longevity, minimal wear, and consistent product quality over millions of cycles.

  • + Multi-cavity & Single-cavity systems
  • + Hot runner & Cold runner integration
  • + High-grade steel selection

Custom Mould Manufacturing

Every product is unique. We provide bespoke tooling solutions based on your specific requirements, whether you have a physical sample, a 2D drawing, or a full 3D product concept. We engineer the mould to fit your exact specifications.

  • + Reverse engineering from samples
  • + Complex part geometries & undercuts
  • + Prototype to mass-production tooling
Bespoke Solutions

Mould Design & Engineering

A flawless mould starts with flawless design. Our engineering team utilizes advanced 3D CAD/CAM software to design the mould, optimizing the cooling channels, ejection systems, and core/cavity layouts to ensure efficient cycle times and defect-free products.

  • + DFM (Design for Manufacturability) analysis
  • + Optimal cooling and gating design
  • + Complete 3D tool modeling
